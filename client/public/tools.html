Registration & Entity Management

SAM.gov sam.gov
Free

Mandatory registration for any company receiving federal contracts. Also the authoritative source for UEI lookup, debarment checks, and exclusion verification. Every contractor must be active here before award.

02

Finding Opportunities

SAM.gov Opportunities sam.gov/search/opportunities
Free

Every federal solicitation over $25K — RFPs, RFQs, Sources Sought notices, and awards. Filter by NAICS code, agency, set-aside type, and dollar threshold. This is where capture starts.

USASpending.gov usaspending.gov
Free

Every awarded federal contract since 2008. Use it for competitor research, agency buying patterns, historical pricing benchmarks, and incumbent identification. The single most powerful BD research tool that most people underuse.

SBA SUB-Net eweb1.sba.gov/subnet
Free

Subcontracting opportunities posted directly by prime contractors. If you're a small business looking to team on a large program, start here. Primes list subcontracting goals, NAICS codes, and contact info.

03

Pricing & Cost Estimating

GSA CALC+ calc.gsa.gov
Free

The best free tool for IGCE labor pricing. Search 230,000+ labor rates from GSA Multiple Award Schedule contracts by job title, experience level, and contract type. KOs use it to build IGCEs; contractors use it to sanity-check their rates.

KOs PMs Contractors
Open Tool → Learn in Finance Module →
BLS OEWS bls.gov/oes
Free

Bureau of Labor Statistics Occupational Employment & Wage Statistics. ~830 occupations × 530 metro areas. Useful for establishing a wage baseline in IGCE development, especially for labor categories that don't appear in GSA CALC+.

KOs Contractors
Open Tool → Learn in Finance Module →
GSA Per Diem Rates gsa.gov/perdiem
Free

Official federal travel per diem by city and county — lodging, M&IE, and incidental rates. Required for travel cost proposals and IGCEs that include travel line items. Updated annually each October.

Regulations & Policy

eCFR.gov (FAR / DFARS) ecfr.gov
Free

Daily-updated electronic Code of Federal Regulations — the authoritative source for FAR, DFARS, and agency supplements. Full text search, redline comparisons, and amendment history. Bookmark this over any unofficial FAR site.

Acquisition.gov acquisition.gov

Official FAR text with linked DFARS, agency supplements, and policy updates from DAU and FAI. Good for navigating the supplement hierarchy and understanding how agency-specific rules layer onto the base FAR.

Market Research & Intelligence

USASpending.gov usaspending.gov — Market Research View
Free

Worth repeating: the award history database is the foundation of federal market research. Find who's winning in your space, at what prices, under which contract vehicles, and how long awards last. This is how smart BD shops do their homework.

Federal Procurement Data System — the underlying data source for USASpending. Use the raw FPDS interface for detailed contract-level queries, action-by-action history, and API access to procurement data for custom analysis.

Training & Certifications

Defense Acquisition University (DAU) dau.edu
Free (Gov)

Free training for DoD civilian and military acquisition professionals. Mandatory DAWIA certification courses, CLPs, and continuous learning modules. If you're a government employee, DAU is your primary certification path — Acqlerate supplements it, not replaces it.

Free (Gov)

Civilian agency acquisition training and FAC-C (Federal Acquisition Certification in Contracting) certification. The civilian equivalent to DAU's DAWIA. If you're a contracting officer at a non-DoD agency, your cert path runs through FAI.

Free open-source AI tools purpose-built for acquisition professionals: MCP servers that connect AI to live SAM.gov data, USASpending.gov, FAR/DFARS, and BLS wage data. Includes a SOW/PWS builder and IGCE builder. Requires ~30 min setup. Best for tech-comfortable users.
