Privacy Policy

We know that people in the defense acquisition space take data seriously. This policy explains exactly what we collect, why we collect it, and what we don't do with it. No legal fog. Questions: hello@acqlerate.com

The short version: We collect what we need to run the platform. We don't sell your data. We don't share it with third parties except the services listed below that help us operate. We don't store sensitive government or program information — we're an education platform, not a data broker.

Payment information

Payments are processed entirely by Stripe. Acqlerate never sees, stores, or handles your credit card number, CVC, or billing details. We store only:

  • Your Stripe Customer ID (a reference number, not payment data)
  • Your subscription status (free, monthly, lifetime)
  • Purchase type (subscription or one-time pack)

Email leads (landing page)

If you submit your email on our landing page to receive a free starter kit, we store your email address and the source of the submission (e.g., "landing page form"). We use this to send you the kit and occasional relevant updates. You can unsubscribe at any time.

Analytics

We use Google Analytics 4 (GA4) to understand aggregate traffic — which pages are visited, how people navigate the site. GA4 data is anonymized. We do not use advertising-based tracking or behavioral retargeting.

2. What we do NOT collect

  • Your security clearance level or status
  • Your military rank, unit, or command
  • Your employer or contractor affiliation (unless you tell us)
  • Any classified or controlled unclassified information (CUI) — don't put it here
  • Your government PIV card, CAC, or ID information
  • IP addresses stored against your identity
DoD users: Acqlerate is a commercial education platform. Do not enter classified information, CUI, or sensitive program data into any field on this platform. Use only unclassified, publicly available information when interacting with the AI study assistant or any other feature.

4. Cookies and session data

We use a single session cookie to keep you logged in. It is a functional cookie — the platform cannot work without it. We do not use advertising cookies, tracking pixels, or cross-site cookies. Google Analytics uses its own cookies per Google's policy; you can opt out via Google's opt-out tool.

5. Data storage and security

Your data is stored on servers hosted by Railway in the United States. Passwords are hashed using bcrypt before storage — we cannot read your password. Access to the database is restricted to authorized systems only.

We use HTTPS for all connections. We do not transmit unencrypted personal data. That said, no system is perfectly secure — if you discover a security issue, please email us immediately at hello@acqlerate.com.

6. Your rights

You can:

  • Access your data — email us and we'll send you everything we have on your account within 30 days.
  • Delete your account — email us at hello@acqlerate.com with the subject "Delete my account." We'll remove your personal data within 30 days, retaining only anonymized records needed for accounting.
  • Correct your data — update your display name and email in account settings, or email us.
  • Unsubscribe from marketing emails — every marketing email includes an unsubscribe link. Transactional emails (receipts, password resets) cannot be opted out of.

7. Children's privacy

Acqlerate is designed for working professionals. We do not knowingly collect data from anyone under 18. If you believe we have received data from someone under 18, contact us and we will delete it immediately.

8. Changes to this policy

If we make material changes, we'll notify you by email and update the effective date above. Continued use of Acqlerate after notice constitutes acceptance.

9. Contact

Privacy questions or data requests: hello@acqlerate.com

We're a small team and we respond promptly.
