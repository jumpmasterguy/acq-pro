Privacy Policy

People in defense acquisition take data seriously, so we tried to write this the way we'd want it written for us: what we collect, why, who else touches it, and how to make us delete it. No legal fog. Questions go to hello@acqlerate.com.

The short version
  • We collect what it takes to run your account, track your progress, and take payments. That's it.
  • We don't sell your data, and we don't use ad networks or ad tracking.
  • A handful of service providers help us run things (hosting, payments, email, sign-in, AI). They're all listed below.
  • You can delete your account yourself, any time, from My Account. It takes about ten seconds.
  • Please don't put classified information, CUI, or sensitive program data anywhere on Acqlerate.

1. Who's responsible for your data

Acqlerate (acqlerate.com and the Acqlerate mobile apps) is run by Lucas Cruz, an individual based in Spain. In privacy-law terms, that makes Lucas the "controller" of your personal data. When this policy says "we," it means Acqlerate and the person running it.

Contact for anything privacy-related: hello@acqlerate.com.

Payments

Payments are handled entirely by Stripe. Your card number, CVC, and billing details go straight to Stripe and never touch our servers. What we keep:

  • Your Stripe customer ID and subscription ID (reference numbers, not payment data)
  • Your plan status (free, trial, monthly, annual, lifetime)
  • For template pack and Team Pack purchases: the email you bought with, what you bought, the amount, the date, Stripe's reference numbers, and a private download link with a count of how many times it's been used

If you sign up for our free starter kit or emails

If you give us your email on a landing page or pop-up, we store the address and where you signed up (for example "landing page"). We use it to send what you asked for, plus occasional updates. Every one of those emails has an unsubscribe link.

When you email us

If you write to us, we keep your message and our reply so we can help you and have a record of what we agreed.

Notifications to us

When someone signs up, joins the email list, buys something, cancels, or deletes their account, we get an email notification with their name and email address so we can say hello, help, or follow up. These land in our Google inbox.

Technical data

Like every website, our hosting and network providers see your IP address and basic browser and device info when you load a page. That's how the internet delivers the page to you, and it's also how we block brute-force login attempts and spam (for example, limiting how many times one address can try to log in). This shows up in short-lived server logs. We don't save your IP address to your account.

Analytics

We use Google Analytics 4 to count visits and see which pages people use. It tells us things like "40 people read the CPARS lesson page this week," along with rough location (country or city) and device type. It also counts a few key moments: a lesson being completed (with the quiz score), and someone signing up, logging in, or starting checkout. We don't send Google your name, email, or account ID, so none of this is tied to you, and we don't use Google Analytics for advertising. If you're in Europe, it only runs if you say yes. Anyone can switch it off with Cookie settings.

3. What you type into our tools

Cost & Burn Rate Tracker

If you use the tracker, we store what you enter: task order and project names and codes, funding modifications (CLIN, SLIN, ACRN, amounts, dates, descriptions), cost entries, and your indirect rates (fringe, overhead, G&A, fee). We store it only so the tracker can show it back to you.

  • It's private to your account. Other users can't see it.
  • We don't analyze it, use it for anything else, or share it with anyone.
  • "Deleting" a project in the tracker hides it (archives it). Everything you've ever entered is permanently erased when you delete your account.

AI Study Assistant and FAR translator

When you use an AI feature, we send the lesson text (and, for the FAR translator, whatever clause number or text you type) to Anthropic's Claude API to write the answer. We don't send your name, email, or account details with it, and we don't store the conversation. Under Anthropic's commercial terms, Anthropic doesn't use this text to train its models. It deletes it within 30 days, unless it's flagged for breaking Anthropic's usage policy, in which case it can keep it longer to deal with the abuse.

DoD and contractor users, please read: Acqlerate is a commercial education platform, not an accredited government system. Don't enter classified information, Controlled Unclassified Information (CUI), export-controlled data, source selection information, or company-proprietary numbers anywhere on Acqlerate, including the tracker and the AI features. Use made-up or publicly available figures for practice.

4. What other learners can see

Leaderboards. Signed-in learners can see leaderboards that show your first name and last initial (for example "Maria G."), your XP, your streak, and your Daily Challenge scores. Never your email or full last name. Don't want to be on it? Turn it off any time in My Account.

Certificates. Your certificates show the name on your account (edit it in My Account), the module, and the date. They're only shared if you share them.

5. What we don't collect

  • Your security clearance level or status
  • Your military rank, unit, or command
  • Your employer's name (unless you tell us, for example in an email or a Team Pack purchase)
  • Your CAC, PIV card, or any government ID
  • Your precise location
  • Anything from your phone's contacts, photos, camera, or microphone

We also don't sell or rent your personal information, don't "share" it for targeted advertising, and don't use it to make automated decisions that have legal or similarly significant effects on you.

8. Cookies and similar storage

  • Sign-in cookie. One cookie keeps you logged in. The site can't work without it. It lasts up to 30 days, and you're signed out automatically once you've been away for 30 minutes (tab closed or in the background).
  • Theme cookie. If you pick light or dark mode, a small cookie remembers it for a year.
  • Google Analytics cookies. Used to count visits, as described above. If you're in Europe (the EU, EEA, UK, or Switzerland), we ask first, and Google Analytics doesn't load at all until you click Accept. Everywhere else it's on by default, and you can turn it off below.
  • Your cookie choice. A small cookie remembers whether you said yes or no, for 12 months, so we don't ask on every page.
  • Stripe. When you check out, Stripe's own page sets cookies it needs to prevent fraud.
  • Your device's local storage. Small settings like "I've already seen the tour," your lesson filter, or the invite code from a friend's referral link (kept for 30 days so it counts when you sign up). These stay on your device until they're needed.

No advertising cookies, no tracking pixels, no cross-site tracking.

Change your mind any time. Saying no has no effect on how the site works for you.

You'll also find "Cookie settings" in the site footer, in the app's sidebar, and at the bottom of My Account. You can also block or delete cookies in your browser, or use Google's opt-out add-on.

9. Emails we send

  • Account emails (welcome, password resets, purchase and download emails, and important notices about your account or these policies). These are part of running your account, so they still arrive if you unsubscribe from everything else. Stripe emails your payment receipts directly.
  • Lesson emails, reminders, and newsletters. A short series after you sign up, a heads-up before your free trial ends, and occasional updates. One click on "Unsubscribe" in any of them stops all of these. We keep a record of unsubscribed addresses so we never accidentally email you again.

11. Your rights and controls

Do it yourself, right now

  • Change your name: My Account.
  • Hide from leaderboards: My Account.
  • Stop marketing emails: the Unsubscribe link in any of them.
  • Delete your account: My Account, on the website or in the app. This permanently deletes your account, progress, tracker data, and email-list sign-up, and cancels any monthly subscription. Purchase records stay for tax reasons, as noted above.

Ask us

Email hello@acqlerate.com and we'll respond within one month (usually much faster). You can ask us to:

  • Access the data we hold about you, or get a copy in a portable format
  • Correct something, including changing your email address
  • Delete your data
  • Restrict or object to how we use it
  • Withdraw consent you've given us

We may ask you to confirm the request from the email on your account, so we don't hand your data to someone pretending to be you. These rights come from the EU's GDPR, but we honor them for everyone, wherever you live.

If you're unhappy with how we've handled your data, you can complain to a data protection authority. In Spain that's the Agencia Española de Protección de Datos (AEPD), or you can go to the authority where you live. We'd appreciate the chance to fix it first, though.

12. Our legal reasons (for EU and UK users)

European law asks us to name the legal reason for each thing we do with your data. Here they are in plain English:

  • To give you what you signed up for (performance of a contract): your account, progress, the tracker, AI features, certificates, leaderboards, payments, and account emails.
  • Legitimate interests (things a reasonable user would expect, balanced against your rights): keeping the platform secure, usage and session data, and lesson emails to people who already have an account. You can object to any of these, and unsubscribing stops the emails.
  • Consent: Google Analytics cookies for visitors in Europe (withdraw any time with Cookie settings), and starter kit and newsletter sign-ups from people without an account (withdraw any time with the Unsubscribe link).
  • Legal obligation: keeping purchase records for tax purposes.

13. Security

Every connection uses HTTPS. Passwords are hashed with bcrypt. Password reset links are single-use, expire after an hour, and only a hash of each link is stored. Accounts are signed out after 30 minutes away, and login attempts are rate-limited. Other learners can never see your private data. Only the site administrator can view account records, and only to run the service and help you. Database access is limited to the systems that run Acqlerate.

No system is perfect. If you find a security problem, please email hello@acqlerate.com right away. If a breach ever puts your data at risk, we'll tell you and the relevant authorities as the law requires.

14. Children

Acqlerate is built for working professionals and you must be 18 or older to use it. We don't knowingly collect data from anyone under 18. If you think we have, tell us and we'll delete it.

15. Changes to this policy

When this policy changes, we update the date at the top. If a change is significant (for example a new kind of data or a new use for it), we'll email account holders before it takes effect.

16. Contact
