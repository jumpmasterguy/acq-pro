For the Defense Acquisition Workforce

CLPs: the 80 hours you owe every two years.

Acquisition workforce members need at least 80 hours of continuous learning every two years, with a goal of 40 a year. Training from outside providers can count, and your supervisor decides the credit. Acqlerate hands you the paperwork that makes it an easy yes.

Common questions

Before you log anything.

CLPs
Will my organization accept Acqlerate for CLPs?

Outside training can count toward continuous learning, and your supervisor decides the credit. Acqlerate is not accredited and is not affiliated with WarU or DoD, so we can't promise a yes. What we can do is give your supervisor everything needed to make the call: a certificate with hours, completion date, functional areas, and an ID they can verify online.

What exactly is on the certificate?

Your name as it appears on your account, the module, the date you finished it, hours of instruction, CLPs, the DAWIA functional areas it maps to, a certificate ID, and a link to acqlerate.com/verify where anyone can confirm it.

Can I get a certificate before I finish the module?

No. The certificate unlocks only when every lesson in the module is complete, and the date on it is the day you actually finished.

Where do I log it?

In whatever system your component uses to track continuous learning, entered as outside or external training. CAPPMIS, eDACM and FAITAS are the common ones. Your DACM office or supervisor will know which is yours.

PDUs
Does Acqlerate need to be PMI-approved for me to claim PDUs?

No. PMI's "Education: Online or Digital Media" category does not require the provider to be a PMI Authorized Training Provider. ATP status matters for instructor-led claims. Self-paced online learning is self-reported, with no provider number or pre-approval.

How does PMI define 1 PDU?

One hour of qualifying professional development. For self-paced online learning, that is an hour of working through structured material: lessons, exercises and assessments. Acqlerate's hours come from the lesson lengths and are reported conservatively.

What if PMI audits me?

Keep your certificates. Each one shows the module, the date you finished and the hours, and it can be verified online. Annual Pro's CLP ledger also exports every certificate to a CSV, which doubles as your PDU record.

Can I claim content I finished before I earned my PMP?

No. PDUs must be earned inside your current 3-year cycle, which starts on your exam pass date.

How many more PDUs will I need after Acqlerate?

About 44.6 PDUs clears the 35 Education minimum. Your renewal is 60 in total, and up to 25 of those can be Giving Back PDUs. PMI also asks for at least 8 in each Talent Triangle area, and Acqlerate's Power Skills share is about 3.4, so pick up the rest of that area elsewhere.

Both
Can the same hours count for CLPs and PDUs?

They are separate systems run by different organizations, so you report to each one under its own rules. Many acquisition professionals who hold a PMP log the same course in both places. Check your component's policy and PMI's handbook if you are unsure.
