PMI Policy

What Counts as a PDU — and Why Acqlerate Qualifies

PMI accepts self-paced online learning under the "Education: Online or Digital Media" category. No PMI ATP (Authorized Training Provider) status is required for this category. The credential holder self-reports, and PMI accepts the submission without pre-approval.

Acqlerate qualifies as "Online or Digital Media" education because it is a structured, lesson-based learning platform with defined learning objectives, module assessments, and measurable completion milestones — not casual browsing or unstructured reading.

No pre-approval is needed. Unlike some PDU categories (such as instructor-led training from an ATP), self-paced online learning does not require you to obtain provider registration numbers or wait for PMI certification. You learn, you log, you're done.

Common Questions

PDU FAQ

Does Acqlerate need to be PMI-approved for me to claim PDUs?

No. PMI's "Education: Online or Digital Media" category does not require the provider to be a PMI Authorized Training Provider (ATP). ATP status is required for Instructor-Led Training PDU claims. Self-paced online learning is self-reported by the credential holder — no provider registration number or pre-approval is needed.

How does PMI define 1 PDU?

One PDU equals one contact hour of qualifying professional development. For self-paced online learning, this is one hour of actual engagement with structured learning material — watching lessons, completing exercises, taking assessments. Acqlerate's PDU values are based on conservatively measured module completion time.

What if PMI audits my self-reported PDUs?

PMI conducts periodic random audits. If audited, you'll need documentation showing the activity occurred. Your Acqlerate account dashboard shows module progress and completion dates — that record serves as your audit documentation. Keep a personal log of completion dates and PDU amounts as a backup.

Can I claim PDUs for Acqlerate content completed before I got my PMP?

No. PDUs must be earned within your current 3-year certification cycle, which begins on your PMP exam pass date. Content completed before your certification date — or in a prior cycle — does not count toward your current renewal requirement.

Does DAU training also count as PMI PDUs?

Yes. DAU (Defense Acquisition University) online training qualifies under the same "Education: Online or Digital Media" self-reporting category. For DoD purposes, DAU completions are tracked in FAITAS; for PMI purposes, you log them separately in CCRS. Many defense PMs combine DAU completions with Acqlerate modules to reach their 35 Education PDU minimum efficiently.

How many more PDUs do I need after completing all Acqlerate modules?

Acqlerate's full Pro curriculum earns ~10.4 PDUs — roughly 30% of your 35 Education PDU minimum. To reach 35, you'll need about 25 more from other sources: PMI events, webinars, DAU courses, or additional online learning. For your total 60 PDUs, up to 25 can be Giving Back PDUs (volunteering, mentoring, content creation), which most active professionals accumulate organically.
