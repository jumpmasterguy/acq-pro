Why We Built This

I couldn't answer a question about my own program.
That's why Acqlerate exists.

The story, the data, and the regular guy behind it.

Read the story ↓ Start Module 1 free

I was sitting in a meeting with people who'd been in the defense industry for years. Someone turned to me and asked about DSO, cashflow, and the variance of revenue for the financial period.

I had no idea what to say. So I gave them the slow, thoughtful nod. You know the one. It says "great question" while your brain quietly looks for the nearest exit.

It wasn't that I couldn't learn it. Nobody had ever explained it to me in plain English. I was a veteran who'd worked on programs worth hundreds of millions of dollars, and I didn't have the vocabulary to talk about my own work.

I don't want that to happen to you. That's why I built Acqlerate. Not to add more jargon to the pile. To give the people doing this job the plain-English foundation to do it with confidence.

P.S. DSO is Days Sales Outstanding: how many days, on average, it takes to get paid after you send the invoice. Past me would have loved that sentence.

The rule I stole from Special Forces

Practice the basics. Every day.

At SFAS (Special Forces Assessment and Selection), one of the cadre asked us what actually makes Special Forces soldiers special. People guessed the cool stuff. Sniper school. Ranger school.

His answer: they practice the basics every day. So when the real fight shows up, it isn't a big moment. It's just another day they've already lived a thousand times in training.
