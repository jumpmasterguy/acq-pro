Why We Built This

I couldn't answer a question about my own program.
That's why Acqlerate exists.

The data behind the gap, and the story behind the fix.

I was sitting in a meeting with people who'd been in the defense industry for years. Someone turned to me and asked about DSO, cashflow, and the variance of revenue for the financial period.

I had no idea what to say. Not because I wasn't capable. Because nobody had ever explained it to me in plain English. I was a veteran who'd worked on programs worth hundreds of millions of dollars, and I still didn't have the vocabulary to talk about my own work.

I don't want that to happen to you. That's why I built Acqlerate. Not to add more jargon to the pile. To give the people doing this job the plain English foundation they need to do it with confidence.

This gap isn't just me. It's the whole industry.

The data backs up exactly what I felt in that meeting. This is a structural gap, and it's expensive.

10+ years
Time acquisition professionals report it takes to reach full proficiency. Most programs can't wait that long for their people to catch up.
Source: MITRE, AiDA Digital Vision
40 of 48

Source: GAO-26-108457, Jul 2026
12+ years
Average time it now takes DoD to deliver a major weapon system capability — GAO ties this directly to thinning program office staff and workforce gaps.
Source: GAO-26-108457, Jul 2026

These numbers describe a shortage of qualified, experienced acquisition professionals, not simply open headcount. DoD can post a position. It can't manufacture ten years of learned judgment overnight.
