Help & Support

Stuck on something? Email hello@acqlerate.com. A real person reads every message, usually Lucas, the founder.

Fastest way to get help
  • Email hello@acqlerate.com from the address on your account.
  • Say whether you're on the website, the iPhone app or the Android app.
  • A screenshot of what you're seeing helps a lot.

I can't sign in

On the sign-in screen, tap Forgot your password? and we'll email you a link to set a new one. The link works for one hour. If you signed up with Google on the website, the same link lets you add a password so you can sign in on the app.

If you used Sign in with Apple, tap that button again on the same Apple account. If you chose "Hide My Email", your account uses an Apple relay address, so look for our emails in the inbox Apple forwards to.

My plan isn't showing in the app

Your plan belongs to your Acqlerate account, not to a device. Sign in on the app with the same email you used on the website. If it still doesn't show, sign out and back in. Still stuck? Email us and we'll sort it out.

AI features

The AI buttons (explain it simpler, Teach It Back, Explain My Mistake, How Do I Apply This?) use Claude, an AI made by Anthropic. The first time you use one, we ask before sending anything. You can turn AI features off any time in My Account. What we send and keep is in the Privacy Policy.

The app says I'm offline

Acqlerate needs an internet connection to load lessons and save progress. Check Wi-Fi or mobile data, then tap Try again. Your progress is saved to your account, so nothing is lost.

I found a mistake in a lesson

Thank you. Email the lesson name and what looks wrong to hello@acqlerate.com. We check every report against the source and fix lessons fast.

Delete my account

Go to My Account, then Delete account, and type DELETE to confirm. This works on the website and in the apps. It removes your account and progress for good and cancels any active subscription. Details are in the Terms of Service and the Privacy Policy.
