# PM Essentials — Your First 90 Days as a DoD Program Manager

**September 2026 Edition · Reflects FAC 2025-06 · $24**

Three files. One workbook does most of the work.

## What's in the box

| File | What it is |
|---|---|
| `pack-guide.pdf` | Plain-English guide to every tool. Read first. |
| `pm-essentials-workbook.xlsx` | The workbook. Eight tabs, all connected. |
| `pm-briefing-deck.pptx` | 12-slide PMR template with leadership questions in every slide's speaker notes. |

## The workbook

| Tab | Purpose | You fill in | It computes |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Start Here** | Orientation, lesson map, license | — | — |
| **Dashboard** | One-page program status for your PEO | Program, PM, as-of date | Money vs. plan, risk counts + every open HIGH, 90-day progress, RACI gaps, traffic lights |
| **First 90 Days** | 30 things to do when you inherit a program: Orient / Assess / Act | Status per item | % complete per phase |
| **Spend Plan** | Monthly obligations & expenditures vs. plan for one FY | Plan + actuals, appropriation, budget authority | Cumulative variance, BEHIND/ON PLAN/AHEAD, % of BA, remaining to Yr-1 obligation goal |
| **Risk Register** | 16 pre-loaded DoD program risks with mitigations | Rescore P × I for your program | Score, level, summary counts |
| **Risk Map** | Your risks plotted on the 5×5 grid | — | Redraws from the register. Print for the weekly review. |
| **RACI** | 25 program decisions, one row each | Names | Every row has an Accountable owner |
| **RACI Instructions** | Role definitions | — | — |

## Build & verify

The workbook is generated by `build_pack1_workbook.py` (repo root, not shipped). Every formula was recalculated in LibreOffice headless with zero errors. Cross-sheet references, the TEXTJOIN array formula on the Dashboard, and all conditional formatting were verified after recalculation.

Sheet protection is on (no password): formulas and headers locked, input cells open. Unprotect via Review → Unprotect Sheet.
