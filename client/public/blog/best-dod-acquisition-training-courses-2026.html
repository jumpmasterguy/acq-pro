DAU / WarU (Warfighting Acquisition University)

DAU (renamed WarU in November 2025) is the gold standard for mandatory DoD acquisition training, and it's free for the government workforce. The catalog is huge, policy-accurate, and DAWIA-aligned.

The catch is delivery: courses run four to forty-plus hours, navigation is clunky, and the phone experience is mostly a rumor. Forty hours is a full work week with one narrator and a progress bar. Plenty of people finish with their CLPs logged and the "why" still fuzzy.

Management Concepts

Management Concepts is where agencies send Contracting Specialists, KOs, CORs, and PMs for instructor-led FAR/DFARS depth. The instructors are strong, the Q&A is real, and courses often count toward CLPs.

It's also $1,500 to $3,500 per course, on a fixed schedule, with no self-paced option and no mobile experience worth mentioning. If your agency is paying, it's excellent. If you're paying, that's a vacation you didn't take.

Coursera and edX (Plus the DAU Coursera Deal)

First, the thing people search for: since 2018, DAU has been buying Coursera licenses for the Defense Acquisition Workforce, free to the learner (agile software, Johns Hopkins data analytics, University at Buffalo digital engineering). It's a nice perk inside the workforce, off-limits outside it, and it has no acquisition content either way. More in DAU vs. Coursera vs. Acqlerate.

The regular Coursera and edX catalogs are affordable, self-paced, and mobile-friendly, but nothing there is DoD-specific. You won't learn how PPBE works, what a DD Form 1423 is, or what separates a CPFF from an FPIF contract in practice. Good education, wrong building.

Acqlerate

Acqlerate is built for people working in or breaking into DoD acquisition who want the understanding, not just the certificate. Fourteen modules and 123 lessons run from contract types to program finance and capture, with novice, intermediate, and advanced tracks so you can skip what you already know.

It's $5.99 a month or $99 for lifetime, mobile-first, and a lesson fits in the time it takes the conference room projector to accept your laptop. The built-in AI study assistant is DoD-specific, so asking about earned value or CMMC gets a defense answer, not an MBA one.

Modules come with CLP credit you self-report, but the catalog is smaller than WarU's on purpose, and it won't replace formal DAWIA certification training.

Free Acquisition Training Courses: What's Actually Free in 2026

Short version: "free" depends on which badge you wear.

If you're in the government acquisition workforce

  • WarU. The whole catalog at no cost. Nothing else comes close for DAWIA.
  • DAU's Coursera licenses. Agile, data analytics, and digital engineering, paid for by DAU. Ask your training office.
  • FAI (fai.gov). The civilian-agency counterpart for FAC-C and FAC-P/PM, free to federal employees.

If you're a contractor or trying to break in

  • WarU, space-available. WarU opens seats to DoD contractors, other federal agencies, and international students when there is room, and instructor-led classes cost nothing. Register through SAAR, then wait behind DoD employees. The general public? "Not at this time."
  • FAI's contractor pointers. fai.gov sends you to WarU seats, GSA Interact, the SBA Learning Platform, and NCMA. Useful, scattered, not a curriculum.
  • Acqlerate's free tier. The full Foundations module (10 lessons) plus the first lesson of every other module, free for good, no credit card. New accounts also get 14 days of everything.
  • This blog. Plain-English explainers on what defense acquisition is, FAR vs. DFARS, and the complete guide. Free is free.

The pattern: the deep, structured, free stuff belongs to the workforce. Everyone else gets free fragments and pays for structure. Management Concepts charges classroom prices; Acqlerate charges less than a sandwich.

Frequently Asked Questions

Is DAU free for contractors?

Free, yes. Available, sometimes. WarU charges nothing for instructor-led or virtual classes and extends seats to DoD contractors on a space-available basis once you register through SAAR. DoD employees get priority, so plan for waitlists. Contractors who need a dependable path usually pair it with Acqlerate or Management Concepts.

What is WarU?

The Warfighting Acquisition University, which is the Defense Acquisition University under its November 2025 name. Same institution, same iCatalog, new domain: dau.edu now redirects to waru.edu. Everyone still says DAU, and nobody will correct you.

Which acquisition training courses are completely free?

For the government workforce: all of WarU, DAU's Coursera licenses, and FAI. For everyone else: WarU seats when available, FAI's public resources, and Acqlerate's free tier (the 10-lesson Foundations module, the first lesson of every other module, and a 14-day trial of everything). The general public can't enroll at WarU right now.

Can I get DAWIA CLPs from Acqlerate?

Yes, as self-reported credit. Finish a module, download its Certificate of Completion, and log it as External Training in WarU. It won't replace the formal DAWIA certification training your career field requires.

Do I need prior acquisition experience?

No. Foundations starts from zero, and every module has novice, intermediate, and advanced tracks. If you already know your CPFF from your FFP, jump to the advanced track and skip the warm-up.
