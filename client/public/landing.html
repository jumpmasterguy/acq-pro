Curriculum Standards

Built on the official blueprints. Not someone's interpretation of them.

Acqlerate's curriculum is mapped to the authoritative DoD frameworks that define what acquisition professionals are actually required to know. We didn't make up the topics. We built to the standard.

DoD DPCAP · Nov 2024
DoD Contracting Competency Model

The official competency framework for the DoD contracting community, covering the full contract lifecycle from pre-award planning through solicitation, source selection, award, administration, and closeout.

Acqlerate modules aligned:
→ Defense Contracting Fundamentals: contract lifecycle, solicitation, source selection, administration, modifications, REAs
→ Capture Management & BD: solicitation development, evaluation criteria, proposal compliance, source selection from buyer perspective
View the official framework →
OPM / USD A&S · DAWIA (Defense Acquisition Workforce Improvement Act)
Contracting Specialist Professional Standards & DAWIA Functional Areas

The qualification standard and functional area competencies for DoD acquisition workforce members, spanning Program Management (PM), Contracting (CON), and Business Financial Management (BFM).

Acqlerate modules aligned:
→ DoD Acquisitions Foundations: acquisition lifecycle, ACAT levels, roles (PM · CO · COR · DCMA), OTAs, FAR Part 12
→ Defense Finance & Budgeting: PPBE, appropriations law, EVM, cost estimating, DCAA oversight (BFM functional area)
→ PM Operations & Leadership: risk management, stakeholder communication, program reviews (PM functional area)
Transparency
What "mapped to" means (and what it doesn't)

Acqlerate is not an accredited institution and is not affiliated with WarU, DoD, or any government agency. "Mapped to" means our curriculum covers the same knowledge domains these frameworks define. It does not mean we have formal approval from these bodies.

DAW members self-report Acqlerate training as External Training in their portal. The frameworks above define what the acquisition workforce must know. Acqlerate teaches it in plain English, at your pace, with a Certificate of Completion for your records.
