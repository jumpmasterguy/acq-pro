OPM / USD A&S · DAWIA (Defense Acquisition Workforce Improvement Act)
Contracting Specialist Professional Standards & DAWIA Functional Areas

The qualification standard and functional area competencies for DoD acquisition workforce members — spanning Program Management (PM), Contracting (CON), and Business Financial Management (BFM).

Acqlerate modules aligned:
→ DoD Acquisitions Foundations — acquisition lifecycle, ACAT levels, roles (PM · CO · COR · DCMA), OTAs, FAR Part 12
→ Defense Finance & Budgeting — PPBE, appropriations law, EVM, cost estimating, DCAA oversight (BFM functional area)
→ PM Operations & Leadership — risk management, stakeholder communication, program reviews (PM functional area)
Transparency
What "mapped to" means — and what it doesn't

Acqlerate is not an accredited institution and is not affiliated with DAU, DoD, or any government agency. "Mapped to" means our curriculum covers the same knowledge domains these frameworks define — not that we have formal approval from these bodies.

DAW members self-report Acqlerate training as External Training in their portal. The frameworks above define what the acquisition workforce must know. Acqlerate teaches it — in plain English, at your pace, with a Certificate of Completion for your records.

Why We Built This

I was in a meeting and couldn't answer the question.

From Lucas

I was sitting in a meeting with people who'd been in the defense industry for years. Someone turned to me and asked about DSO, cashflow, and the variance of revenue for the financial period.

I had no idea what to say. Not because I wasn't capable. Because nobody had ever explained it to me in plain English. I was a veteran who'd worked on programs worth hundreds of millions of dollars and I still didn't have the vocabulary to talk about my own work.

I don't want that to happen to you. That's why I built Acqlerate. Not to add more jargon to the pile. To give defense contractors the plain-English foundation they need to do the job with confidence.
