# Theme: INFOMERCIAL

Genre feel: late-night TV pitch. Black-and-white "struggling with the old way" shots, a too-happy host,
"But wait, there's more", a price that keeps dropping, tiny disclaimer text read very fast. Never name a real product or show.

Beat sheet (about 55 to 75 seconds):
1. COLD OPEN (broll, narrator as the pitchman): "Are YOU tired of ...?" Overlay title = two short lines.
2. "THAT'S ME" (dialogue): the character, struggling in black and white, raises a hand: "That's me!" Name card: NAME / SATISFIED CUSTOMER.
3. THE PRODUCT (broll, narrator): the shortcut, presented like a miracle gadget (the thing the lesson warns against).
4. TESTIMONIAL (dialogue): the character gushes about how easy it is.
5. DEMO (broll, narrator): watch it "work": the character doing the lazy version of the task.
6. "BUT WAIT, THERE'S MORE" (dialogue): the character doubles down with an even worse idea.
7. THE BIG PROMISE (dialogue): peak confidence, banner with the fake number/benefit, then FREEZE + record scratch.
8. THE DISCLAIMER (dialogue, second character, deadpan, reading fast): the real rule, in fine print. music stops before.
9. THE WOBBLE (dialogue): "...it says what?"
10. RESULTS NOT TYPICAL (broll, off-screen second character): the consequence. Name card strike-through, new title: RESULTS NOT TYPICAL.
11. REWIND (narrator): "Rewind!"
12. TEACH (4 narrator lines, drawn board): the real rule. Question -> evidence -> comparison cards -> punch line -> the number that changes.
13. "CAN I RETURN IT?" (dialogue): one pleading beat.
14. DON'T BE <NAME> (card): stamp + three checklist actions.
15. CTA (narrator): "<Lesson title>. <Module>, on Ack-luh-rate."
16. LAST WORD (dialogue): the character's sheepish sign-off.
