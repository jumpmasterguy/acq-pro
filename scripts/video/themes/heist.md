# Theme: HEIST

Genre feel: slow-motion hallway walks, cork boards with red string, "I've got a crew", the plan,
the champagne, the one deadpan person who read the fine print. Never name a real film or show.

Beat sheet (about 55 to 75 seconds):
1. COLD OPEN (broll, narrator): a trailer-style hook line. Overlay title = two short lines.
2. "THAT'S ME" (dialogue): the character claims the hero role. Name card: NAME / THE <ROLE>.
3. THE PRIZE (broll, narrator): what they won / got / signed. Then a whispered dialogue beat worshipping it.
4. THE PLAN (broll, narrator): the shortcut they are about to take. Then a proud dialogue beat ("I've got a CREW!").
5. THE CREW AT WORK (broll, narrator, no main character): other people doing the real work.
6. THE SHORTCUT (broll, narrator): what the character actually does instead. One smug dialogue beat.
7. THE CELEBRATION (dialogue): peak confidence, big number on screen (banner), then FREEZE + record scratch.
8. THE DEADPAN (dialogue, second character): the quiet professional delivers the bad news. music stops before this beat.
9. THE WOBBLE (dialogue): the character's tiny "...I'm not?"
10. THE DEMOTION (broll, off-screen second character): the punchline reframe ("You're the getaway driver."). Name card strike-through.
11. REWIND (narrator): "Rewind!"
12. TEACH (4 narrator lines, drawn board): the real rule. Question -> evidence -> comparison cards -> punch line -> the number that shrinks.
13. "CAN WE REWIND AGAIN?" (dialogue): one pleading beat.
14. DON'T BE <NAME> (card): stamp + three checklist actions, each 2-4 words, imperative.
15. CTA (narrator): "<Lesson title>. <Module>, on Ack-luh-rate."
16. LAST WORD (dialogue): the character's sheepish sign-off.
