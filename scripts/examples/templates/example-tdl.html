2.0 Purpose

This Technical Direction Letter (TDL) provides technical clarification in support of implementing the Distributed Network Support Operations (DNSO) Task Order on behalf of Allied Forces Command, Meridian Theater (AFC-MT).

3.0 Project Period and Place of Performance

Forward Operating Site Alpha and various installations throughout the Meridian Theater AOR. On-site personnel will work within the confines of Government installations, when able, and utilize Government property. Period of Performance (PoP) associated with this TDL is from 01 November 2025 through 31 October 2026.

4.0 Project Summary

The objective of this TDL is to provide AFC-MT reliable, multi-site, cross-domain network and IT infrastructure capabilities that increase system availability, enhance the supported command's operational posture, increase partnership capacity and interoperability, and foster stability throughout the assigned AORs. This project connects distributed network nodes, data centers, and communications links across all sites to enable reliable command and control at the scale and tempo needed to accomplish commander's intent.

This project is broken into the following primary effort: procure and sustain equipment to support the network infrastructure mission, and provide operator training on equipment procured under this task.

5.0 Technical Clarification

Note: real TDLs only include the numbered tasks that apply to that specific TDL. The skipped numbers below (5.2, 5.7, etc.) are intentional, not missing content. A TDL never renumbers the master task list from the Task Order. It just cites the subset it's activating.

5.1 Task 1: Program Management (TO Section C.5.1)

The contractor shall provide program management support, including management and oversight of all activities performed by contractor personnel, including subcontractors, to satisfy the requirements identified in this TDL.

5.3 Task 3: Deployed Site Support and Logistics (TO Section C.5.3)

The contractor shall provide deployed site support and logistics services across four forward locations within the AFC-MT AOR: Forward Site Alpha, Forward Site Bravo, Forward Site Charlie, and Forward Site Delta. This task area includes conducting Pre-Deployment Site Surveys (PDSS) (Section F, Deliverable 22) and coordinating logistics for equipment shipping/receiving with the military distribution system.

5.4 Task 4: IT Infrastructure and Systems (TO Section C.5.4)

The contractor shall provide research, design, development, engineering, installation, integration, testing, upgrades, and maintenance services in support of network infrastructure systems, including network switching, satellite communications, data links, and lifecycle maintenance of IT equipment (Section F, Deliverable 26).

From Lucas This is the real shape of a TDL. Notice it doesn't create new scope or add funding on its own. It clarifies HOW you execute against work that's already inside the Task Order. If a TDL tries to add brand-new tasking you can't trace back to a TO section, that's a red flag: scope creep on official letterhead. Compare this to the IAC, MAC and GWAC hierarchy chart in this lesson. The TDL lives at the very bottom of that chain, directing execution within an existing task order's boundaries.
