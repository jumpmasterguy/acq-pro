From Lucas Notice the MSR isn't just a status update. It's a contract deliverable with a due date (the 10th calendar day of the month) tied to Section F. The financial section at the bottom is what your Government customer reads first. Every time. If your ETC and EAC don't move logically month over month, a sharp COR will flag it before their coffee gets cold. Keep your MSR numbers consistent with what you're reporting on your invoice. They should tell the exact same story.
