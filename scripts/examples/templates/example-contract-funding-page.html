Contract Structure: The Uniform Contract Format (UCF)

Every negotiated federal contract that uses the Uniform Contract Format (FAR 15.204-1) is built from the same set of lettered sections, grouped into four parts. The funding page you are about to read lives inside Section B, highlighted below, but knowing where it sits relative to the rest of the contract is what turns "reading a table" into "reading a contract."

Section B is short on its own (it is mostly a pricing table), but it never stands alone. Section C tells you what the work actually is. Section F tells you when it is due. Section G tells you who administers the money. Section B is simply where the price tag for all of that gets attached, broken out by CLIN.

Each CLIN below is its own funded line item, with the full set of fields a real Section B carries: the SOW reference, Product Service Code, Pricing Arrangement, ACRN, PR Number, and the Contract/Funding Instrument Number (CIN). "Firm Price" is the total negotiated value of that CLIN. "Funded Amount" is the dollar value actually obligated right now, the number a contractor can legally bill against today.

How to Read This Page

  • CLIN 0001 is fully funded: Firm Price and Funded Amount are identical ($771,600). The full value of this line has been obligated up front.
  • CLIN 0002 is incrementally funded: Firm Price is $706,800, but only $530,100 has actually been obligated so far, 75.0% of the total. That is not a coincidence for this example: it is deliberately set at the FAR 52.232-22 notification threshold.
  • CLIN 0003 (Travel) is fully funded like CLIN 0001, showing that funding status is set per CLIN, not per contract.
  • ACRN AA covers CLINs 0001 and 0003, meaning they draw from the same funding action. ACRN AB is a separate funding line covering CLIN 0002 only, likely a different fiscal year or a later funding modification.
From Lucas CLIN 0002 is the one to watch on this page. A PM tracking the $706,800 ceiling thinks there's plenty of room left. A PM tracking the real number, the $530,100 Funded Amount, already knows they've hit the 75% threshold. The written Limitation of Funds notification to the CO is due now, not after the money runs out. Ceiling tells you what's possible. Funded Amount tells you what's real. Track funded. Every time.
