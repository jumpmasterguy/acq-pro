What it means for a PM

  • Two years of lead time. Money you need in FY{{FY}} had to be in the POM built in {{FYm2}}. A late requirement waits a cycle or competes for reprogramming.
  • Fight in the programming window. Spring and summer of the POM year is when priorities move. In execution you manage what you got.
  • Under a CR: no new starts and no production-rate increases unless Congress grants an exception.
  • Obligate early. One-year money not on contract by Sep 30 is gone for new work.
Reform watch. The Commission on PPBE Reform (final report, March 2024) called for more flexibility, and DoD has since asked for higher reprogramming limits and some two-year O&M. Check the current appropriations act for what Congress actually enacted.
